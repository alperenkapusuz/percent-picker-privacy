Percent Picker – Privacy Policy

Percent Picker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or usage data.

The extension runs only in your browser. When you turn it on, it reads the position and size of the page element you select and the position of your clicks in order to calculate percentage and pixel values. This information is displayed on the page and, when you use the copy feature, placed on your clipboard. It never leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
